> *"This is our AI Operations Command Center. Right in the center is the 3D visual representation of ResolveAI operating a digital customer service environment.*  
> *Notice the 8 tool nodes surrounding the core: **CRM 360**, **Order Ops**, **Payments Ledger**, **Refund Engine**, **Ticket Core**, **Comms Dispatcher**, **Telemetry**, and the **Human Supervisor**.*

### 3. Scenario 1: Delayed Order $\rightarrow$ Autonomous Refund & Verification (1:30 – 2:30)
*Action: Click **RUN DEMO → Scenario 1: Delayed Order** (or click the 'Delayed Order' quick button).*
> *"Let's watch a real workflow. A customer submits: 'My order hasn't arrived. Please refund me.'*  
> *Watch the 3D Core react in real-time:*
> 1. *First, the Core shifts into **ANALYZING** as particles accelerate.*
> 2. *It shoots data packets to **CRM 360** to verify the customer profile — Rahul Sharma, Platinum Tier, trust score 94/100.*
> 3. *Next, it queries **Order Ops** — shipment ORD8842 is delayed 6 days past the delivery SLA.*
> 4. *Now, the **Deterministic Policy Engine** activates. Rule POL-402 authorizes a full refund because the amount (₹2,499) is well within our ₹5,000 autonomous threshold.*
> 5. *It executes the payout through the **Refund Engine**.*
> 6. *CRITICAL DIFFERENTIATOR: It enters **VERIFYING**. A golden halo wraps around the core as it reconciles the bank payment gateway receipt.*
> 7. *Once verified under Gateway Ref `REF-PAYTM-99214`, it notifies the customer on WhatsApp and SMS, and closes the ticket.*
> *Zero human intervention. Zero hallucinated outcomes. 100% verified.*"
